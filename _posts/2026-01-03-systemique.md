This is my first attempt at "proper" vibe coding. Not just copying and pasting AI-generated snippits, but going full on into cursor and barely touching the code. It did need some tweaks, but for the most part, this turned out great.

Systemique is a diagramming tool for linking pre-defined concepts together. The key being pre-defined - they have interfaces and rules already, and you can only connect things up in certain ways. It's an architecture tool with some opinions, and a (hopefully) eye-pleasing aesthetic. My design, AI code. It's using vue.js and vue-flow for the heavy lifting, with not much else. It's clean, fast, and has a good library of parts. Have a play [here](https://lgdan.github.io/systemique/) and check it out [the source code](https://github.com/LGDan/systemique).
