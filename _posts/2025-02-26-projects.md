date: 2025-02-26

---

I'm not a person who gets bored. There's always something to do, some idea on the mind, or a project I can dust off and pick up. Once I used to play games, now I do projects.

Do you ever get that guilty feeling when just chilling out? "I could be doing something productive now..." or "I could be learning something new...". That's also me. I only play games when I'm super spent now. Projects are the main hobby, and there are many.

I aim to do one a week, and by that I mean the project needs to be in a finished enough state by Sunday for me to play with it or use it to do something else the following week. No half-finished stuff. Even if it's an alpha with some basic functions and no polish, that's enough. It has to work. Demo ready.

Not only is the Sunday deadline a bit of motivation, but it keeps the scope tight and focused, and constrains what I set myself to deliver to be bite sized.
